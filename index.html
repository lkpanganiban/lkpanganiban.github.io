01 / About

A data developer
at heart.

Hi, I'm Ian Panganiban — a data developer working the full spectrum of data. I like to build things and solve problems in the digital world, with a passion for open source, open data and open knowledge.

I'm a machine learning engineer at OpenSolar and co-founder & CTO at Ausome Maps, building a platform for special education facilities and practitioners. Before that I spent six years at Iraya Energies across software development, data science and cloud infrastructure.

12+ years of experience. Based in Metro Manila, Philippines. Open to interesting problems, collaborations and good conversations.
